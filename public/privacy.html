Chrome Kush Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026. Chrome Kush is published by BURNETT'S Eggs LTD.

What we collect

  • Game progress (levels, scores, coins, lives, boosters, login streak) is stored on your device. In the Amazon Appstore version it is also saved to our server under your anonymous Amazon user ID so you can recover it after reinstalling.
  • Purchase receipts: when you buy something, we send the Amazon receipt ID and anonymous Amazon user ID to Amazon to confirm the purchase. We never receive your name, email, or payment details.

What we don't do

  • No account, location, contacts, camera, or microphone access.
  • No third-party ad or analytics trackers. Ads shown in the game are our own.
  • We don't sell or share personal data.

Age

Chrome Kush has a mature cartoon theme and is intended for adults (18+). It is a game only and does not sell or promote real cannabis products.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling removes local data. To delete your cloud save, contact us with the request and we will remove it.
